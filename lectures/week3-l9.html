Principal Component Analysis (PCA), also called Empirical Orthogonal Functions (EOF), helps reveal major patterns by decomposing varying data into fixed spatial patterns multiplied by their time-varying amplitudes.

TOP3 methods used methods to reveal modes of variability in ocean & earth sciences!

Preprocessing Data for PCA/EOF

1. Remove the mean to retain only variations:

Depending on your purpose, this step could include removing seasonal cycles or a long-term trend (fit a line using OLS and calculate anomalies relative to the fitted line at each location).

Because PCA/EOF finds the strongest mode first, retaining seasonal cycles will often make the first mode describe how your data varies seasonally.

2. Reshape data into a 2D matrix:

Anomalies are reshaped into a matrix with dimension space × time.

You typically need .reshape() and .transpose().

Weight of data on long-lat grids before EOF analysis

Area of a grid cell ∝ Δlon x Δlat x cos(lat)

EOF analyses the variance of data. So the variance of data should be weighted by cos(lat) to ensure unit area has equal weight.

Essentially, this is to weigh data by the square root of cosine latitude.
