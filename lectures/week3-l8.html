Assumptions:
Residual errors (not Data) are Normally distributed and independent from one another.

Prob(Omnibus)

H₀: residuals are Normal. p < 0.05 is a warning that they may not be Normal.

Durbin-Watson

Close to 2 suggests no residual autocorrelation. Values near 0 or 4 are warnings.

Skew

Close to 0 is more symmetric. Positive values indicate a longer right tail.

Prob(JB)

Another residual Normality test based on skewness and kurtosis. Interpret its p-value in the same way.

Kurtosis

A Normal distribution is approximately 3. What we learned in L6 is this value minus 3.

Cond. No.

No universal cutoff. A value much larger than 30 is a cue to inspect scaling or collinearity.

Since complex models tend to generalise worse, we can penalise complex models by adding a penalty term to the loss function, such that these models are less preferred.

BIC = Loss + Penalty

Lower BIC is preferred because it reaches
a balance between a better fit and a simpler model.
