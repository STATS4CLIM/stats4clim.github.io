Statistics helps us to uncover patterns from data, develop understandings, and inform decision making. Yet it is not only about extracting knowledge from data; it is also about understanding uncertainty. Both will be crucial for your future careers as ocean and earth scientists.

Learning by doing

Each topic is paired with a case study using real ocean and climate data. We will not dwell on mathematical details, but a basic level of mathematics is still required.

In the AI era, the key skill is not only to obtain an answer, but to recognise when an answer is wrong, where the assumptions may fail, and what should be checked before trusting the result.
